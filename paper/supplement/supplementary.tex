\textbf{Experimental conditions.}
The four networks are Bio-DMELA, DBLP, Higgs social, and Orkut.
E1 and E2 use total privacy budgets from 0.5 to 4 in steps of 0.5.
E3 varies the region boundaries at budgets 0.5 and 2. PrivTDD uses the allocations in Table~\ref{tab:e1:budgets} and sets the transport safeguard parameter to $\tau=1$.
% PrivTDD uses the budget allocations in this table and transport-safeguard parameter $\tau=1$.

\textbf{Metrics.}
Let $H(t)$ count vertices participating in exactly $t$ triangles, and let $F(t)$ be
the fraction participating in at most $t$; hats denote estimates. For $n$ vertices,
$W_{\max}=\binom{n-1}{2}$. We report the mean squared error
$\mathrm{MSE}=\sum_t(\widehat H(t)-H(t))^2/(W_{\max}+1)$, the earth mover's
distance $\mathrm{EMD}=\sum_t|\widehat F(t)-F(t)|$ on integer counts, and the
Kolmogorov--Smirnov distance $\mathrm{KS}=\max_t|\widehat F(t)-F(t)|$.
Smaller values indicate more accurate estimates. Absolute means are in the original units.

\textbf{Uncertainty.}
Brackets give pointwise 95\% percentile bootstrap intervals from 2,000 resamples
of completed run indices within each network and budget (random seed 731).
For a paired contrast, each draw uses the same indices for the two estimates.
Positive differences indicate greater error in the first named variant.
Run counts refer to method outputs or matched report sets as specified in each table.
In the detailed paired-difference tables, the interval appears directly below its mean.

\section{Overall estimation accuracy (E1)}\label{sec:e1}
Table~\ref{tab:e1:absolute} gives all four methods at all eight budgets. We scheduled
20 runs per method and budget on Bio-DMELA and DBLP and ten on Higgs social and Orkut.
We obtained 1,916 of 1,920 planned method outputs. Two PrivTDD fits on Higgs social at $\varepsilon=0.5$
and two on Orkut at $\varepsilon=4$ did not converge. Each mean uses the runs
available for that method. PrivTDD attained the lowest mean EMD in 30 of the 32
network--budget settings, the lowest KS in 29, and the lowest MSE in 23.

\subsection*{Additional component comparisons}
The two reference ablations remove either the $\beta$ or the geometric (Geo) reference
and refit the remaining strengths. Mass-only and shape-only retain their
respective correction directions. GlobalMix fits two global mixture weights.
The final two variants omit posterior reweighting or the transport safeguard.
% The final variant omits the transport safeguard.
Table~\ref{tab:e2:extra-means} gives both mean errors for the matched runs.
Table~\ref{tab:e2:extra-paired} gives the uncertainty in their paired difference.

Each reference ablation has 411 matched runs, and each of the other five
ablations has 410. Bio-DMELA, DBLP, and Higgs social cover all eight budgets.
For Orkut the available additional runs cover $\varepsilon=0.5$ (ten per variant)
and $\varepsilon=1$ (three per reference ablation and two per other variant).
Those last intervals summarize only two or three observations. Each variant uses
its own matching full runs. On DBLP at $\varepsilon=2$, removing either reference
raised EMD, from 2.651 to 4.268 without $\beta$ or 5.834 without Geo.
On Bio-DMELA the corresponding means were 2.032, 2.024, and 1.903.

\subsection*{Location of the DBLP error change}
For each run we evaluated all three fits on the same ranges, defined by that
run's default integer boundaries $b_1,b_2$. The four EMD contributions sum
$|\widehat F(t)-F(t)|$ at $t=0$, $1\le t\le b_1$, $b_1<t\le b_2$, and $t>b_2$.
Moving from the default to the upper pair reduced the mean tail contribution
from 1.697 to 1.004 and raised the body contribution from 0.503 to 0.902
(Table~\ref{tab:e3:diagnostic}). We chose $t=2$ for inspection after finding it
was the upper pair's KS peak in nine of ten runs. The true fraction of vertices
in at most two triangles was 37.38\%. The mean estimate rose from 43.11\% at the
default to 47.25\% at the upper pair.
